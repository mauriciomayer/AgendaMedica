# Reconciliation — docs/Agendamento Medico.txt vs prd.md

## Coverage

Every functional point in the source's "Funcionalidades Confirmadas" list is represented in the PRD, generally with a clear RF and testable consequences: Android-only v1 (§Plataforma), 15-minute slots (Glossário, RF-2, RF-5), 48h minimum lead time (RF-6), 24h cancel/reschedule window by patient or doctor (RF-8), total cancellation block after 24h with explicit no-exceptions framing (RF-9, plus a dedicated "Notas" callout marking it a deliberate, already-validated scope decision — this nicely preserves the source's own reasoning, not just the rule), doctor self-registration without validation and immediate search visibility (RF-1), doctor defines one specialty + convênios + schedule (RF-2), fixed convênio list with no edit/removal even with existing appointments (RF-2, carried through correctly), search by specialty and location (RF-4), push/SMS/email reminders (RF-10), schedule-conflict locking (RF-7), no in-app payments (Monetização, Non-Goals), and no external integrations / no telemedicine / no formal LGPD requirement (all explicitly listed in §5 Não-Objetivos). The core-complexity narrative from the source (the three interlocking rules — 48h lead, 24h cancel window, concurrency lock — as the technical heart of the system) is well captured in §1 Visão and echoed again in §RNFs Transversais ("toda a Complexidade Média do projeto vem dele"), which explicitly cites the source's "Complexidade Média" label. The one known approved deviation (Convênio list expanded from 3 to 4 with "Particular") is applied consistently everywhere the list is referenced (Glossário, RF-2, Não-Usuários, Não-Objetivos, Escopo do MVP) with no leftover inconsistency.

## Gaps

- The source's dedicated "Risco" section ("Baixo. Todos os pontos anteriormente indefinidos foram resolvidos, o volume é pequeno, não há dependências externas, e as regras de negócio ficaram simples e diretas (sem exceções a tratar)") has no counterpart anywhere in the PRD. Unlike "Complexidade Média," which is explicitly quoted once in §RNFs Transversais, the risk rating and its rationale are not restated in any form (not in Visão, not in RNFs, not in a risks/assumptions section). The underlying facts (low volume, no external dependencies, no exceptions to handle) are scattered across the PRD, but the qualitative risk conclusion itself — a distinct judgment the source explicitly recorded — is silently dropped.
- The source closes with a confident, decisive tone: "Pontos em Aberto: Nenhum ponto crítico permanece em aberto. O escopo está pronto para orçamento e planejamento técnico," reinforced by the Resumo Executivo's "100% definido." The PRD does not echo this closure statement anywhere, and in fact runs in a slightly different key: §8 Perguntas Abertas lists two open items (SMS/push/email technical provider; how the externally-developed visual design will be formally incorporated). These are legitimate process items introduced during PRD discovery (not gaps in the original closed scope — the source never named a specific SMS provider or discussed visual design at all), so this is not a contradiction, but the PRD never states outright that the original functional scope itself remains fully closed/undisputed the way the source does. A brief line reaffirming that closure would keep the source's tone intact.
- Minor/low-severity: the source's explicit causal framing "20 usuários/semana — permite arquitetura simples no MVP" (volume directly justifying a simple architecture) is only indirectly reflected in the PRD's RNFs Transversais ("sem exigência de otimização para escala maior em v1," "não há exigência de alta disponibilidade formal") rather than stated as a direct implication the way the source frames it. Low priority, but it's a slightly different rhetorical framing than the source used.

None found. The only structural change from the source's closed scope — the Convênio list gaining a 4th "Particular" option — is the pre-approved deviation and is applied consistently.
